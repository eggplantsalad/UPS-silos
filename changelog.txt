Version: 1.0.0
Date: 2026-10-29
  Features:
    - Updated for Factorio 2.1. Silos can now be rotated freely while
      building, dragging, or in blueprints/copy-paste. Once placed, a
      silo can only be flipped 180 deg, not turned a full 90 deg - a
      standard engine restriction for non-square buildings.
    - Dragging the generic remote now places the silo immediately,
      correctly sized and oriented, instead of needing a second click -
      and no longer undersizes a sloppy drag.
  Changes:
    - Removed the old rotate-key item-swap hack - superseded by native
      rotation above.
    - A "tall" drag now always produces a horizontal silo rotated 90 deg
      instead of a separate vertical item/entity. The vertical items and
      entities still work fully for anything already placed, blueprinted,
      or copy-pasted from before this version, but nothing new creates
      them any more.
    - Factoriopedia cleanup: removed the legacy vertical entries;
      each horizontal size is now a single page instead of a
      duplicate pair, and its recipe shows the real generic-chest cost
      instead of a misleading empty one.
  Info:
    - Upgrading an existing save: already-placed silos and research state
      are unaffected.

Version: 0.2.0
Date: 2026-09-08
  Features:
    - Collapsed the 53 separate craftable silo items down to a single generic
      item, carried and dragged to size/orientation on the map (1xN or Nx1
      selection, up to 27 tiles) - the specific sized silo is resolved right
      there at build time, not chosen ahead of time at the crafting menu.
      No more stocking 27+ dedicated items per orientation: a requester
      chest or a bot's own cargo needs only the one generic item, in a
      quantity matching whatever mix of sizes it's building, and manual
      (non-bot) building works the same way.
    - Rotating (the same key as vanilla's rotate) while holding a silo item
      or ghost-aim swaps it for its same-size opposite-orientation
      counterpart, since horizontal and vertical are separate entities
      rather than direction variants of one. Doesn't affect blueprint
      contents - see the README's Known limitations.
    - Silos now show the vanilla slot-limiter bar in their GUI, letting you
      cap how many of the 10 slots are usable, same as any other chest.
  Changes:
    - Deconstruction always collapses a silo back into the generic items,
      never a specific sized one.
    - Bots prefer building from an owned specific item when available,
      falling back to the generic item (linear in size) otherwise.
    - Pipetting (Q) a ghost or an already-built silo redoes the same
      generic-to-specific trade.
    - Remote view only ever produces a free ghost-aim when pipetting, never
      manifests or consumes a real item, since there's no physical body
      present to hold one there.
  Info:
    - The 53 specific sized items are meant to exist only momentarily, in
      the cursor, mid-placement. The stray-item reclaim only watches the
      player's own main inventory, so a specific item can still persist
      elsewhere if it's moved directly into another inventory without ever
      passing through the main one - e.g. shift-click-transferred straight
      into a chest, a vehicle trunk, a train wagon, or another player's
      inventory, or picked up off the ground by a construction bot or
      spidertron rather than a player. This is harmless (the item is fully
      functional and still correctly builds its silo) but means it won't
      automatically collapse back into the generic item in those cases.
    - Upgrading an existing save from 0.1.0: already-placed silos are
      unaffected - entity names didn't change. Old specific sized items
      (crafted under the old per-size recipes) remain valid and placeable
      too; any sitting in a player's own main inventory will automatically
      convert into the equivalent generic items the next time that
      inventory changes, while any sitting elsewhere (a chest, a vehicle,
      bot cargo) are left alone and stay usable as-is. The old per-size
      recipes themselves no longer exist at all though - any assembling
      machine set to craft one will lose that recipe selection and needs
      to be manually reassigned to craft the generic item instead.
